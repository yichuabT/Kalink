Networking Fundamentals

what is a modem?
A modem is a device that modulates and demodulates signals to enable communication over a particular transmission medium, commonly connecting a home or business network to an internet service provider.
what is a wireless access point (WAP)?
A wireless access point allows wireless devices such as smarthphones and laptops to connect to a wired network using WiFi.
wht is LAN?
LAN stands for Local Area Network. it connects devices within a limited geographical area, such as a home, school, office, or building.
what is a MAN?
MAN stands for Metropolitan Area Network.It connects multiple local networks across a city or metropolitan area.
what is a PAN?
PAN stands for Personal Area Network. It connects personal devices over a short distance, shuch as a smartphone connected to wireless headphones through Bluetooth.
what is the main difference between LAN and AWN?
LAN covers a relatively small geographical area, while WAN connects networks over much larger geographical distances.
which type of network is commonly used to connect computers within a school computer laboratory?
LAN. A computer laboratory usually connects devices within a limited geographical area.
which network type can connect the branch offices of a company located in different countries?
WAN. A Wide Area Network allows geographically distant networks to communicae.
what is OSI model?
The Open Systems Interconnection (OSI) model is a conceptual framework that devices network communication into seven layers to help understand and standardize how data travels between devices.
How many layers does the OSI model have?
Seven layers. Physical, Data Link, Network, Transport, Session, Presentation, and Application.
